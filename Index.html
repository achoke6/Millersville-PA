Common Questions

How does pricing work?

We offer flexible monthly plans tailored to your business size and goals. Contact us for a custom quote — no commitment required.

Can I submit a free community event?

Yes! Community events from established local organizations are included at no cost. Business promotions and commercial events are part of our paid options.

How quickly will my listing go live?

Most listings go live within 24 hours of approval. Featured placements update automatically every hour through our data pipeline.

I'm a restaurant but I'm not listed yet. Can I be added?

Absolutely! Submit your details and we'll add you to the Food page. Upgrade to Featured for gold-highlighted placement with ordering links and daily specials.

What makes this different from Facebook or Google ads?

Your listing lives where Millersville residents already go for local info — events, games, food, and news. It's hyper-local, always visible, and not buried in a social media feed.

Can I update my listing or specials myself?

We handle all updates for you. Just send us the changes and we'll have them live within hours. Restaurant specials can be updated weekly.
