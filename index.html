Common Questions

How does pricing work?

FREE! Advertise your events or daily/weekly food specials. Reach out and we'll go over the details with you.

How do my events and specials get on the app?

Integration with your website, spreadsheet, or shared calendar are some of the options we use. We wire it up once and keep it current. No new busywork for you.

Can I submit a one-off event myself?

Yes — anyone can submit an event right from the app using the event submission form. A paid listing is for businesses that want their specials and events kept current automatically, plus audience targeting and a verified spot in the Directory.

What is audience targeting?

You pick who sees your listing — local townies, MU students, or both — so you're reaching the right crowd.

I'm not listed yet. Can I be added?

Absolutely. Click the Get Listed button above and we'll get you set up with a verified listing.

How is this different from Facebook or Google ads?

You live where people already come for local info — not buried in a feed, not fighting an algorithm. Always there, always local.

How fast does my listing go live?

Usually within 24 hours of setup. Your events and specials refresh automatically after that.
